# Direção visual e revisão

A Home preserva o carrossel, as três coleções e os oito produtos do roteiro do curso. O catálogo conserva a barra de filtros e a grade. No detalhe, a galeria fica ao lado da área de compra no desktop e acima dela no celular. Consulte as [referências de layout do curso](https://github.com/digitalcollegebr/projeto-digital-store/tree/main/doc/layout).

A direção usa marfim, verde-oliva e terracota, com Fraunces nos títulos e DM Sans na interface. O texto de cada coleção identifica os tipos de produtos que a pessoa vai encontrar. A revisão com Unslop verificou o conteúdo comercial, a legibilidade e a coerência das fotos em telas desktop e móvel. As fotos adicionais da Poltrona Brisa foram refeitas com base na sua imagem de catálogo.

## Prompts finais do ImageGen

Composition foreground chair and sideboard centered to right. Earthy moss, ivory and terracotta accent. Frame furniture fully, no people, zero text, no logos. This is the lead photograph of a well designed Brazilian housewares store.

### Coleções

> A 3-panel strongly aligned triptych photo contact sheet, three equally wide vertical photos side by side with absolutely no margin or panel gutters. Left third a comfortable moss green lounge chair and side table in a contemporary Brazilian living room. Middle third a warm oak dining table laid with matte ceramics under natural window light. Right third a nightstand, linen bed and textured pillows in a warm minimalist bedroom. Each scene genuinely camera-shot, curated natural woods and linen, warm plaster, soft daylight. No text, no typography, no people, no marks. Landscape 3:2 overall.

### Catálogo

> Catalog product photography contact sheet EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows, square canvas, NO gridlines NO bars NO gaps, equal square tiles, spaced with neutral warm ivory backgrounds. One separate realistic isolated product centered inside each quadrant with generous breathing room and natural light. Row 1 left to right: curved moss green upholstered lounge armchair, cream modern 3 seater sofa, oval low oak coffee table, simple solid oak dining chair. Row 2: ivory ceramic table lamp with white lampshade, pair of terracotta sculptural ceramic vases, pale sand textured area rug rolled and folded, beige linen throw pillow. Row 3: solid oak dining table, matte ivory individual stacked dinnerware plates, wall mounted rounded mirror with oak frame, wide oak sideboard. Row 4: warm oat linen bedding bundle, dark walnut nightstand, round handwoven basket, wide olive green modern storage cabinet. No words or graphics. Straight grid for exact crops and all shadows contained within each cell. Contemporary Brazilian interior store.
